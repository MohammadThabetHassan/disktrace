# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

DiskTrace aims to be a respectful, constructive, and privacy-conscious project for people improving recovery and digital-forensics tooling. Participation in the project should be free from harassment and should respect the sensitivity of data-loss, incident-response, and evidence-handling work.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate in good faith, focus criticism on ideas and changes rather than people, respect differing experience levels, protect private and sensitive material, and accept correction when a recovery claim or safety boundary needs to be narrowed.

Use synthetic, minimized examples whenever possible. Do not expose another person’s disk image, recovered material, credentials, personal information, legal matter, or security-sensitive details without clear authorization.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private material, publication of credentials or real recovered data, encouragement of unlawful access, knowingly deceptive technical claims, and repeated disregard for documented safety boundaries.

## Enforcement

Until the project has a public private-contact route, report conduct concerns through the maintainer contact published with the first public release. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports should describe the behavior, where it occurred, and any context needed to investigate it.

Maintainers should review reports promptly, protect the reporter’s privacy where possible, and take proportionate action. Actions may include a clarification, request to change behavior, removal of content, temporary restriction from project spaces, or permanent exclusion from participation.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, project-related communication, and representation of the project in public forums. It complements, but does not replace, the project’s [security policy](SECURITY.md), [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md), and applicable law or organizational procedures.
